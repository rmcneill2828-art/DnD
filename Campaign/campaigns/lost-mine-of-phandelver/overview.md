# Lost Mine of Phandelver

**Type:** Module -- *Lost Mine of Phandelver* (D&D Starter Set), played solo with Claude as DM
**Starting level:** 1 (the adventure runs to about level 5)
**Tone:** Classic heroic fantasy on a rough frontier -- danger on the road, a town that needs
help, old ruins and older secrets. Room for humour and warmth as well as hard fights.

## Premise
The party has been hired in the city of Neverwinter by Gundren Rockseeker, a dwarf with big plans,
to haul a wagon of mining supplies south to the frontier town of Phandalin. Gundren rode on ahead
with a warrior named Sildar Hallwinter to see to business in town; he asked the party to follow
with the wagon and deliver the goods to Barthen's Provisions, where he'll pay them on arrival.

## Starting Hook
A few days out of Neverwinter, the wagon leaves the High Road and turns east along the Triboar
Trail toward Phandalin, half a day's travel away.

## Setting Notes
- The Sword Coast, in the Forgotten Realms. Neverwinter is the great city to the north; Phandalin
  is a small, newly resettled town built over the ruins of an older one, with no lord or army to
  keep the peace.
- The roads between are thinly travelled, and bandits and worse are known to prowl them.

## Known Factions
- Discovered in play. (Several well-known organisations of the Sword Coast have members and
  interests in the region.)

## How this campaign is played
- Solo: the player runs their own character; Claude is the DM (see `dm-notes.md`, which is for
  Claude) and may run companions, if the party has them.
- The story runs in a Claude Code session with this campaign repo; Campaign OS 3D is the battle
  map (Claude plays the monsters with Run Monster's Turn; F8 reports any problem with the app).
