# The Hollow Tide

**Type:** Homebrew (Forgotten Realms, Sword Coast -- Baldur's Gate and the Sea of Swords)
**Ruleset:** D&D 5e (2014), Xanathar's subclasses allowed
**Starting level:** 1 (upper range open -- the whole party multiclasses)
**Tone:** Swashbuckling mystery. Rooftops, rapiers, harbour fog and a conspiracy underneath.
Fights are frequent and have teeth; not everyone can be talked down. Wit, nerve and a plan
matter as much as steel.

## Premise
Lucan Marlowe, Helja Torunn and Kairon Vesk run a small crew out of the Lower City of Baldur's
Gate: clever cons and quiet thefts that never really hurt anyone -- just enough to survive, and a
little extra for those who have less. They have a table at the Leaky Lantern, where they play
cards, fleece tourists with gambling games and listen for the rich and powerful whose cruelty
makes the poor suffer. Kairon always wants the bigger score; Helja wants the gang to take only
from those who've earned it. Lucan keeps the peace with a grin, and keeps a secret of his own.

Something is wrong in the Lower City. A pattern of low tides, sleepless nights and people who
aren't quite themselves. Whatever it is, it's bigger than anything the crew has ever cheated.

## Starting Hook
A job that should have been easy goes wrong on a night with no moon and an odd, sucking low tide.

## Setting Notes
- **Baldur's Gate** is a rich, crowded, corrupt port: the Lower City (docks, taverns, the poor),
  the Upper City (nobles, merchants, temples), the Outer City, and the Flaming Fist, a mercenary
  company that doubles as the city's army and watch.
- The sea is the city's fortune and its fear. Umberlee, goddess of the sea, is appeased rather
  than loved.
- **Calendar:** the campaign opens at the start of Kythorn. Time matters -- things get worse if
  the crew stalls.

## The Crew
- **Lucan Marlowe** -- Half-Elf Rogue/Bard, the player character.
- **Helja Torunn** -- Mountain Dwarf Cleric (Ilmater)/Paladin, DM-run.
- **Kairon Vesk** -- Tiefling Warlock/Sorcerer, DM-run.

## How this campaign is played
- Solo player with two DM-run companions. Dice rolled by system randomness (PowerShell).
- Persuasion is a real option but never automatic: some enemies can't be reasoned with, and a
  bluff that works once costs you the next time.
- The campaign's secrets live in `dm-notes.md` (spoilers -- DM only).
