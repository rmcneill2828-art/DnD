# Session Log -- The Hollow Tide

_Sessions are appended as `## Session N -- date`, in narrative prose (named beats, real decisions)._

_Character creation was completed on 2026-10-14 (Session 0)._

## Session 1 -- 2026-10-14 (1 Kythorn, 1492 DR)

**Party:** Lucan Marlowe (Half-Elf Rogue 1, played), Helja Torunn (Mountain Dwarf Cleric 1), Kairon Vesk (Tiefling Warlock 1).

- **The Leaky Lantern.** Lucan fleeced a drunk Waterdhavian, Corvin Aldersey, with weighted dice. Aldersey's
  friend Brannick saw the switch; Lucan talked his way out and the last hand tied honestly. Brannick said he'd
  be "keeping an eye on" him.
- **A letter in green wax** from Lucan's mother Isaura (with "Come. -- G." from his father): dinner tomorrow at
  seven, at the family house, with a guest -- Master Vossen of the Harbourmaster's office. Lucan read it in
  private and told no one.
- **The tide went out and stayed out.** A thin clerk in the Lantern, smiling and wet, walked out toward the
  water. Old Mother Pell mentioned dockhands on Wharf Row going quiet "since Ches", coming home kind and empty.
- **Warehouse Nine, Wharf Row.** Kairon's tip. Helja talked the watchman home. On the slip, an old night
  watchman, Tam Harrow, stood barefoot at the mud's edge waiting for the water: a "Mother of Tides" beneath the
  harbour, "singers" in grey, the House of Salt, and a night when the water goes all the way out. Lucan took his
  brass key. Helja led him away to the hospice.
- **Inside:** a dozen emptied dockhands carrying drowned crates up from a flooded hatch under the eye of Mister
  Quill, who writes names in a ledger. Something spoke through Quill: it knew Lucan's "cadence from the water",
  invited the crew to the House of Salt, and said to "tell the one with the ring it was a fair price."
- **The door broke.** The Hollowed followed Quill, not the crew. Lucan led the whole silent line six streets to
  Ilmater's hospice on Candle Row; Sister Hesper coaxed them over the threshold and they slept. Quill murmurs
  names in his sleep.
- **The ledger.** Hundreds of names marked *Rested*; an INTENDED list including Margery Pell (9 Flamerule),
  Helja ("the singing sister: obstruction") and Kairon ("Hold. The Mother's own. Do not take."); a line about
  Marlowe & Sons, Berth 14 ("levy paid in kind"); and THE DRY NIGHT, 30 Eleint, ringed. A loose chart marks
  "the Founders' lock" off the Seatower of Balduran.
- **Giant crabs** came up out of the hatch while Lucan was reading. A critical hit dropped him to 0. In the
  last seconds Kairon begged his patron for help, and it answered.

**End state:** Lucan 1/10 HP, tasting salt, with a memory missing that he hasn't found yet. Kairon 10/10, his
Hex slot spent. Helja at the hospice with Tam, Quill and eleven sleeping dockhands. Ledger and chart in hand.

## Session 2 -- 2026-10-14 (2 Kythorn, 1492 DR)

**Party:** Lucan Marlowe, Helja Torunn, Kairon Vesk. All at full strength after a long rest.

- **Candle Row, before dawn.** Kairon dragged Lucan into the hospice; Helja healed him. Lucan felt the
  gap in his mind: "a fair price". Helja read the ledger aloud: her own name ("the singing sister:
  obstruction"), Pell on 9 Flamerule, Kairon as "the Mother's own", and "Marlowe & Sons, Berth 14".
  Kairon confessed that he had called his patron and it had answered. Lucan thanked him and forgave
  him (the funniest and most painful thing Kairon has heard).
- **Rest.** They slept through the morning at the hospice. Lucan dreamed of a wharf at low tide, a
  grandfather with no face and "never go down when it's low". On waking, **Quill had gone**, barefoot
  toward the Fishmonger's Stair, with the ledger taken from under Helja's pillow.
- **The Marlowe house.** Lucan climbed to the Upper City in his midnight-blue coat. He met his father
  Gerard, his mother Isaura and their guest **Aldric Vossen**. Vossen wears a **black pearl**, is clearly
  afraid, and wants Berth 14 and a "quiet talk" with Lucan there, alone, at noon two days hence.
  Lucan rattled him by hinting at "the Mother"; Vossen saw straight through it and warned the family
  about "gossip".
- **The study.** Gerard revealed the family bargain: Augustin Marlowe took something from beside a
  black stone at Berth 14; each spring a Marlowe lays a hand on the stone and leaves blood and a
  memory ("the levy paid in kind"). Gerard stopped in Ches and heard the voice say it would need "the
  next of my blood". The stone answers only to Marlowe blood and the signet ring. Gerard gave Lucan
  Augustin's **salvage log** (a cipher; a chapter on "the House of Salt"). Vossen's men watch the
  house and Lucan's Tallow Street garret. Isaura has been **sleepwalking to the door** with wet feet.
- **The Lantern.** Lucan lied well about the coat and the name ("a distant, rejected cousin"), told his
  friends about the Marlowe link and Vossen's pearl, and proposed guarding the house.
- **Midnight in the Marlowe kitchen.** Isaura rose at the stroke, "Is it time?" and went for the
  cellar door; the sea breathed up the stairs. Helja held her; Lucan broke his cover and called her
  Mother. Helja's hymn broke the pull. Helja knotted her red cord and a salt line across the cellar.
  Kairon realised Lucan is a Marlowe. Lucan asked him to imagine owning it all, and what he'd want.
  Lucan told him what the house was really like: lessons from six, a book on his head, no play.

**End state:** All three at full HP and slots. The party is in the Marlowe kitchen, a little past midnight on
the night of 2-3 Kythorn. Lucan's cover is gone with Helja and Kairon. Isaura is asleep upstairs; Gerard
and Barrow know everything. Vossen expects Lucan at Berth 14 at noon on 4 Kythorn.
