# DM NOTES -- THE HOLLOW TIDE  (SPOILERS -- DM ONLY)

_Player: please don't open this file. It holds the villain's plan, the twists and the ending I'm
steering toward. Everything the player needs is in `overview.md`._

## 1. The two destinations

**A. What the player must stop -- the Dry Night.**
At Highharvestide (the end of Eleint) the harbour of Baldur's Gate drains for a single night, an
event the sailors call the Dry Night. On that night the elder aboleth **Ulthuun the Undertow**,
sealed beneath the harbour since the city's founding, breaks its prison and "drinks" the city's
minds. Everyone it has already Hollowed becomes a conduit, and the waking population is swept in
after them. If it succeeds, Baldur's Gate becomes the first city of a drowned empire of thralls,
and the Sword Coast follows.

**B. The ending I'm steering toward -- the Last Con.**
The party can't beat Ulthuun in a straight fight: it's a lair of mind-slaves, tide-magic and an
ancient intelligence that reads thoughts. What can beat it is a *con*. At the climax, Lucan
performs the biggest lie of his life: he sells the Undertow the story it most wants to hear (that it has already
won) while Helja and Kairon steal the thing that holds it together and break the seal's
counterpart. The campaign's long jokes about talking his way out of trouble pay off here, because
this time the talk has been *prepared*, *earned* and *costs something*.

The crew's old argument (Kairon's "bigger scores" vs Helja's "only from those who deserve it")
must be resolved in the vault: the haul is the greatest treasure any of them has seen, and they must
decide what to do with it. Kairon must decide whether his patron is a friend (see section 4).

## 2. The truth of the Hollowing

- **Ulthuun** (elder aboleth, CR 10 plus lair; see section 7) has been sealed in the **Drowned
  Vault** under the Seatower of Balduran for centuries by Balduran's followers. The seal is a
  vault-lock whose **Tidestone** keystone is failing.
- For two decades it has reached up through the failing seal, through dreams, into the Lower
  City. On the lowest tides, the vulnerable, lonely and desperate sleepwalk into the harbour and
  return **Hollowed**: pleasant, empty, obedient, smelling of brine, remembering nothing of who
  they were. Hollowed are not evil; they're puppets.
- Its human servants are the **Drowned Choir**, a cult of Umberlee that believes the whispers are
  the Storm Lord's own voice. They aren't. The cult's leader is **High Tide-Mother Maren
  Coldwater**, a true believer.
- The money and cover come from **Harbourmaster Aldric Vossen**, a cold, cynical collaborator who
  trades "volunteers" for salvage rights and the aboleth's knowledge of drowned treasure.
- **The mind-store:** everything Ulthuun has stolen is anchored in the Tidestone's twin, the
  **Remembrance**, a black-pearl keystone in the vault. Destroy it carelessly and the Hollowed die
  or go mad; steal and *restore* it, and they remember who they are.

## 3. The clock (Hollowing stages)

Advance a stage as the party's investigation lags or major events pass. Start: Kythorn.

| Stage | When | What the party sees |
|-------|------|---------------------|
| 1. Murmurs | Kythorn | Odd low tides; 1 in 20 Lower City folk "sleep badly"; strangers with salt on their lips |
| 2. Hollowed in the open | Flamerule | Whole families Hollowed; Flaming Fist denies it; sea-creatures in the sewers |
| 3. The city turns | Eleint, first half | Guard captains and magistrates Hollowed; Choir comes into the open; docks sealed |
| 4. The Dry Night | Highharvestide | The harbour drains; the vault opens |

If the party stalls, advance the stage anyway -- people they know get Hollowed (Old Mother Pell
is the likeliest).

## 4. The hidden thread: Kairon's patron

Kairon nearly drowned as a boy in the harbour. Ulthuun reached out and left a *thread* of its mind
in him. The "Great Old One" he serves **is Ulthuun**, or a piece of it: it has been quietly nudging
him toward "bigger scores" for years, the very scores that put the crew in position to find the
Hollowing, because it needs thieves who can get to its vault's twin lock, from the inside.

- Not a betrayal Kairon chose. The aboleth *lies* to him, and he believes the voice is a friend and
  a prisoner of the cult.
- The reveal comes at the end of Act 2. His choice (sever the pact or ride it) is the emotional
  centre of Act 3. His **Sorcerer** side (innate power, not borrowed) is the answer: he never
  needed the patron.
- Ensure Helja's faith and Kairon's ambition both bite: Helja suspects first and says nothing.

## 5. Structure (levels are per-character total)

### Act 1 -- Small Fish (levels 1-3, ~5-6 sessions)
Street-level mystery. Introduce the Lower City, the crew's life, Old Mother Pell, Sergeant Dunmarrow.
- **Opening job:** a break-in at a warehouse the crew has cased (Kairon's tip). The "mark" turns out
  to be a Hollowed clerk; the warehouse holds crates of drowned treasure and salt-crusted ledgers.
- First fights: dockside thugs; Choir cultists; skum in a flooded cellar.
- Lead: a name (Vossen), a tide-table with the Dry Night circled, and a missing sister (Vessa Kroll's).
- First meeting with **Vessa Kroll**, the Silver Gull: a duel Lucan *loses* (not to the death).

### Act 2 -- Tide and Salt (levels 4-7, ~8-10 sessions)
Wider city, the Upper City, and out to sea. Each of Vossen's circle is an arc.
- The Upper City: a masked ball at Vossen's estate (infiltration + social combat).
- The Choir's shrine under the Wyrm's Crossing temple of Umberlee (dungeon).
- Out to sea: a derelict merchantman full of the Hollowed (ship fight); smugglers on **Gull Rock**.
- The reveal that the Choir is Ulthuun's puppet. Kairon's patron reveal ends the act.
- Vessa Kroll becomes an ally mid-act (she's seen her sister Hollowed).

### Act 3 -- The Drowned Vault (levels 8-12+, ~8-10 sessions)
The race to the Dry Night.
- Recruit allies: Flaming Fist loyalists, Pell's network, the harbour's honest sailors.
- Find the **Founders' Key** (Balduran-era) hidden in a sunken galleon off the Reaching Reefs.
- The city turns (Stage 3). Choir in the open; running street battles.
- Set the con: prepare the **Last Con** (see 1B) and test the weak points.

### Act 4 -- The Dry Night (finale)
Heist-and-battle on the drained harbour floor; Ulthuun's lair; the Last Con.

## 7. Encounters and monsters (reference, by tier)

- **Tier 1 (L1-3):** Thugs (commoners/bandits), cultists, giant crabs, skum (use as "Aboleth
  thrall": MM aboleth variants), sahuagin scouts.
- **Tier 2 (L4-7):** Sahuagin raiding party, merrow, giant sea creatures, Choir priests,
  Vessa (Duelist -- Swashbuckler stat block or "Spy"), Hollowed mobs (commoners, with a Dominate-
  like compulsion).
- **Tier 3 (L8-11):** Sahuagin baron, merrow packs, water elemental, Maren (Priest / Tempest
  Domain), banshee-like drowned dead (wights).
- **Boss:** Ulthuun -- Aboleth (MM p. 13) with lair actions and Legendary Actions (CR 10). Reduce
  or lift HP if the party is short on levels; the Last Con should *skip* much of the fight.

## 8. Negotiation guardrails (the player noted past fights ended too easily by talking)

- **Hollowed:** no will to persuade; they need to be *saved*, not argued with.
- **Choir zealots:** will not betray the cause; at most, fear and confusion.
- **Hired killers:** need a price higher than the contract plus the cost of breaking it.
- **Monsters:** no reason; no language or no interest.
- **Cowards (Vossen):** talk readily, but lie; there's always a catch.
- **Vessa:** can be won only through a duel she respects.
- Persuasion DCs scale with what the NPC stands to lose (15 -> 20+). A bluff leaves a trace.

## 9. Mystery clue web (three clues per revelation)

1. **Who is hollowing the Lower City?** (a) salt on lips and wet footprints at dawn; (b) the tide
   table with Dry Night circled; (c) Pell's lodger sleepwalks toward the harbour.
2. **Who's paying for it?** (a) Vossen's salvage licences; (b) the crates of drowned treasure;
   (c) a Hollowed clerk's ledger.
3. **What's under the harbour?** (a) Balduran-era seal marks on the Seatower; (b) old dockers'
   songs; (c) a captured sahuagin who speaks of "the Mother of Tides".
4. **Kairon's patron:** (a) the telepathy "echoes" at low tide; (b) Helja's dream-warning;
   (c) a matching mark in the vault.

## 9b. Session 1 deviations (what actually happened)
- Ulthuun spoke through Quill (earlier than planned) and referred to Lucan's "cadence" and "the one with
  the ring". It knows the Marlowe cadence from Lucan's grandfather. Kairon heard.
- Giant crabs ambushed Lucan; a critical dropped him to 0. Kairon called his patron. **Ulthuun saved Lucan:**
  the crabs withdrew at its word and Lucan woke at 1 HP. **Price taken:** a memory, unnamed. Decide what it
  is (suggest: the memory of *why* he left House Marlowe, or his grandfather's face) and reveal it when it
  hurts. **Kairon owes "a fair price"** (see section 4). Use this as the first lever on Kairon.
- The ledger lists Helja as "obstruction" and Kairon as "the Mother's own". Helja is a priority target.
- Brannick (Aldersey's friend) is a loose thread; use him as a recurring watcher or rival.
- Process lesson: do not batch several combat rounds of rolls for the party's side; run one roll at a time.

## 10. Hooks per character
- **Lucan's secret (set by the player, 2026-10-14):** he's a scion of **House Marlowe**, a wealthy
  Upper City trading family; the crew doesn't know. His parents keep summoning him. **Tie-in:**
  House Marlowe holds harbour contracts and salvage shares through Vossen's licences, so his family
  is entangled in the Hollowing -- knowingly or not. Use it for:
  - A dinner scene where a parent is quietly Hollowed (Stage 2-3) or already complicit.
  - A mid-campaign exposure: Kairon realises Lucan has money and power he never used, and wants
    it used; Helja is hurt by the lie. This is the crew's real fracture, and the place their
    reconciliation is earned.
  - Ensure the Last Con needs *Lucan's name and access* (the Marlowe seal, the family's harbour
    berth) -- the thing he tried to leave behind is the key to the heist.
  - Kairon reads "risk low" as caution; truth is Lucan is protecting the secret and the crew.
  - **Grandfather's past (player-set):** the Marlowes are respectable now, but the grandfather broke
    laws to build the house. Make it the *same* kind of crime the Hollowing feeds on: his first
    fortune came from salvage rights on a wreck he shouldn't have touched near the Seatower, and
    the family's old harbour berth has a Balduran-era seal mark that matches the vault's. The
    "debt" the Marlowes owe is the thing that makes Lucan's name a key (see the Last Con).
- **Lucan:** the Silver Gull's duel; a reputation he's proud of that could make him a target.
- **Helja:** her Ilmater hospice fills with the Hollowed; a chance to save them or be broken by them.
- **Kairon:** the bigger scores; the voice; the sorcerer's choice.
