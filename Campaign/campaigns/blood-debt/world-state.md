# World State — Blood Debt

_Living tracker — active threads only. Full history lives in `session-log.md`; this file should always be quick to scan._

## Party
- **Location:** At **the Standing Ring** with the full inner circle — Vosk, Threnn, Kaldur, Kethrun, plus the household. Thyra holds real acting command of Urskelde in the meantime.
- **"The count," confirmed and fully shared (Session 31).** The whole theory was laid before the inner circle for the first time — Kethrun confirmed "the count" is a real term from his makers' era: a reckoning the ancient enemy keeps only on the rare few who might become something it can't afford to ignore, and that a child turned into his own corruption (rather than simply killed) is how such a debt gets collected cleanly. Vosk, pressed to think back, realized the raid that took Rurik as a child took nothing else — no goods, no other captives — and struck the one night guaranteed to catch him alone: design, not luck. **Rurik then asked the bear-spirit to bond with him fully rather than merely shield him** — disarmed and unarmored at the chamber's center, held through the test via a deliberately balanced rage, and completed the working as a true, equal partnership rather than one-sided protection. New standing ability: advantage on saves against being scried/tracked/divined, plus once per long rest the bear-spirit can speak one truth directly through him. New mark on his forearm alongside the old claw scar. **Kept strictly within this inner circle — not even Sesha and Harek know.**
- **The core, extracted and read (Session 30).** A combined four-bond working (Rurik, Mara, Sael, Ysolde) read a location impression from the captured construct's core: a distant, cold, hollow place, carved from repurposed old stone, "mountain country." Something on the far end responded to the working's *nature*, not any of their identities — anonymity holds. Halvard is running a parallel search on this lead.
- **A live capture, and direct contact with the enemy (Session 29).** A bird-like construct captured alive and held hooded under triple guard back at Urskelde. Rurik spoke directly through a second creature's active scrying link, naming himself as the true target — the connection flinched and cut out, the first real evidence for "the count," not just inference. Kaldur has quietly doubled Urskelde's watch since.
- **The full council, reformed governance (Session 28).** Radical unfiltered reporting from every section head adopted as standing practice; a randomized drill policy; a combined sky-iron workshop (Perrin/Greta/Threnn's smiths); reporting lines set (defense→Kaldur, building→Threnn, supply→Joss, anything larger→Rurik). Garrison recruit Corim Ashvale tasked with a corruption-detection training proposal. Full account: session-log.md.
- **The Still Throat's guardian search, widened** beyond goliath-kin per Kethrun's own confirmation — patient search underway, rotating paired watch (Threnn's people + Thundertree) holding the site, **under Thyra's command**.
- **Greyfeather confirmed as an organization, not one killer** — three unexplained "fated" deaths in Ravenshollow over two years. Craft-signature ties Greyfeather, the corrupted guardians, and the scout creature to one source, also explaining the precise Ashpit targeting order. Investigation formally merged under Halvard.
- **Thyra's public trial (Session 28)** — tested by Rurik in a real sparring match, proven ready; now holding genuine acting command of Urskelde while the party's away. Full account: session-log.md.
- **The Still Throat, resolved with real loss (Session 27)** — the fourth and final site's original guardians were found corrupted into undead Frostbound Wardens and destroyed; the true guardian, Borvik, was lost for good after an overreach. The wound there opens down, not up. Stopgap-warded; needs a permanent, freely-chosen guardian. Full account: session-log.md.
- **Rurik's bond to the sentinel network** lets him reach it directly and unassisted, sensing trouble at connected sites from a distance — deepened further by Session 31's full bear-spirit bond.
- **House Corvane's betrayal, resolved (Session 24).** Lord Edrin Corvane publicly confessed to arranging the Ashpit hit as inherited duty to **the Raven Queen**, not personal malice. Stayed at Urskelde as a partner (see Key NPCs).
- **Kaldur formally named Commander of Urskelde**, though currently traveling with the party — Thyra holds acting command.
- **The ancient goliath-kin village:** Threnn's people (~3 dozen) fully relocated to and settled at Urskelde.
- **Darkhawk and Mara are married** (Session 16) — see character sheets.
- **Duskgate is fully resolved** — anchor broken, bargain ended, market cleared. **The succession mechanic remains the live security concern:** Vale had groomed **Merrick** as a successor without telling him; both held under complete, tested wards at Urskelde. **Malphestor's fate is unknown.**

A running pattern, not a fixed rule: Darkhawk has moved between ruthless (killing surrendering guards at the mine-road ambush, no-quarter at the fairground building) and merciful (turning the waystation and first relay stop rather than killing, freeing captives with a genuine choice to flee or fight, taking Vale alive) depending on the moment and who's in front of him — usually erring toward mercy when someone stops actively fighting, but not always. **The defining choice of the campaign so far, at Duskgate:** rather than kill Malphestor, Darkhawk engaged it honestly, learned the anchor (not the devil) was the real target, and risked his own soul to break it instead — leaving Malphestor alive and telling it "if anger can fall, maybe devils can rise."
