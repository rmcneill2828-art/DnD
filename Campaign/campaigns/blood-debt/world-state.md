# World State — Blood Debt

_Living tracker — active threads only. Full history lives in `session-log.md`; this file should always be quick to scan._

## Party
- **Location:** At **Urskelde**. All four sealed sites accounted for; a full council (Session 28) has just reshaped how the settlement governs itself. The hunt (Session 29) produced a live capture — a bird-like construct now hooded and held under triple guard in the cells, no communication permitted near it. Ysolde examines it daily with Sael present at all times, both for safety and because Sael accompanies every session per Rurik's standing request.
- **A live capture, and direct contact with the enemy (Session 29).** Mara's patrol find (a fresh, wrong print pacing a fixed watch-line near the northern tree line) fed straight into Sael's dusk scouting: two bird-like constructs found, the larger one restrained by Sael's *Entangle* and bound alive by Rurik — Urskelde's first live specimen instead of another cold trail. Left alone with the smaller one, Rurik noticed an active scrying signature behind its eyes and spoke directly through it to whatever was listening: *"It's not the settlement that interests you, it's me. I know this for certain."* The connection flinched violently and cut out; the creature, left undirected, showed no real intelligence (confirmed by a careful Animal Handling read — instinct only, no language) and was released rather than killed. **Kaldur has quietly doubled the garrison's watch** in response, expecting no immediate strike but treating a cornered, patient enemy as a real risk.
- **Rurik's sentinel-network bond can do more than communicate, with the bear-spirit's active help.** A deliberate test the same evening: unassisted, it only returns the network's broad status; with the bear-spirit actively lending itself to the reach, it extended to a faint directional sense of the shard-taint's "wrongness" — accurate enough to give Sael a real head start via Ysolde's *Sending*. Untested whether this scales further or needs the bear-spirit's help every time.
- **The full council, reformed governance (Session 28).** Rurik opened by having every Urskelde section head report first, unfiltered — good and bad both. A labor gap on the new eastern fields (farmer Petra) was solved on the spot with a 10-person rotating field detail. **A standing, randomized drill policy** was adopted — internal and alliance-wide, unpredictable timing, results reported each council. **A combined sky-iron workshop** (Perrin, Greta, Threnn's smiths) was approved, aimed at structural ward-work beyond gear. Reporting lines set: defense to Kaldur, building to Threnn, supply to Joss, anything larger to Rurik. Garrison recruit **Corim Ashvale** was given a real assignment — proposing how to train the garrison to spot subtle "wrongness" in people, not just places — due at the next council.
- **The Still Throat's guardian search, widened.** Kethrun, consulted live during council (Rurik's third, and easiest yet, unassisted reach), confirmed the old alliance bound guardians by free choice of character, not bloodline — the search is not limited to goliath-kin. Council settled on a patient search rather than a rushed volunteer, backed by a **rotating paired watch** from both Threnn's people and Thundertree. **Thyra was given command of the rotation**, with Threnn's elders (Bettha) added to her ongoing education alongside Kaldur and Idra.
- **Greyfeather confirmed as likely an organization, not one killer** — three unexplained "fated" deaths in Ravenshollow over two years, no consistent description across witnesses. The recovered Still Throat shard matches both the Urskelde cairn shard and a centuries-old Ravenshollow cult's spirit-binding craft, tying Greyfeather, the corrupted guardians, and the scout creature to one source. Also explains the impossibly precise Ashpit targeting order. **The Greyfeather and scout-maker investigations are now formally merged** under Halvard, with Kessick's Ford's intelligence folded in.
- **Thyra's public trial (Session 28).** Rurik staged a full-pace, real-stakes sparring match in front of a gathered crowd — deliberately not telling her in advance, making clear beforehand she'd face "Darkhawk," not her brother. She lost most exchanges but landed one genuine touch, then watched him rage fully (controlled, disarming her, blade stopped short) to feel the real gap firsthand. He knelt to give her the moment; she pulled him up instead. Closed with the household naming their own "balancing emotion" against fear/rage — Rurik's own answer: love, crediting Mara directly.
- **The Still Throat, resolved with real loss (Session 27).** The fourth and final site — sealed in haste, the worst-off of all four. Its four "guardians" turned out to be its actual original guardians, corrupted into undead **Frostbound Wardens** and destroyed in a hard fight at the pass. The site's true guardian, **Borvik**, survived only as fragments; contact was made, but an overreach trying to connect him to the full network scattered him for good. His last words: the site's own wound **opens down, not up** — different in kind from Kethrun's skyward tear. A black-iron shard matching Urskelde's cairn shard confirms one enemy behind every site, reaching through multiple doors. No guardian remains to hold this site — a group stopgap ward was cast (expected to last days to weeks) rather than leaving it unprotected; a **permanent replacement guardian, freely chosen rather than another sacrifice, is an open question** for the council.
- **Rurik's bond to the sentinel network has deepened into something new.** After serving as conduit for Ilsevet's and Vessyn's reweaving, he found he could reach the thread directly and unassisted — Kethrun confirms this makes him something like a living tending-hand for the network itself, not just a visitor. He can likely sense trouble at connected sites even from a distance. He raised, then chose not to attempt, opening the Still Throat's downward path himself — held as a known option, not used alone.
- **House Corvane's betrayal, resolved (Session 24).** Lord Edrin Corvane publicly confessed to arranging the Ashpit hit as inherited duty to **the Raven Queen**, not personal malice. Offered redemption rather than vengeance, he stayed at Urskelde as a partner (see Key NPCs). The council pledged a full regional alliance against the threat above Malphestor.
- **Kaldur formally named Commander of Urskelde**, full authority to act. Threnn stands as his political adviser; Halvard's spy network reports to both.
- **The ancient goliath-kin village:** Threnn's people (~3 dozen), released from a centuries-long protective freeze, have fully relocated to and settled at Urskelde.
- **Darkhawk and Mara are married** (Session 16) — see character sheets.
- **Duskgate is fully resolved** — anchor broken, bargain ended, market cleared. **The succession mechanic remains the live security concern:** the bargain needs a living successor to physically reach Duskgate to renew; Vale had groomed **Merrick** for this without telling him. Both are held under complete, tested wards at Urskelde. **Malphestor's fate is unknown** — purposeless, not hostile, hinted at something further up the chain that felt every banked soul vanish.
- **Thundertree losses at the entrance (Session 14):** three warriors dead (Ren Alderhawk, Bram and Torin Stonebrand). Yorik Stonebrand gave Rurik a mourning-stone; Ren's widow Vessa and daughter Kessa asked only that he be remembered as a friend.

## Player Choices of Consequence
A running pattern, not a fixed rule: Darkhawk has moved between ruthless (killing surrendering guards at the mine-road ambush, no-quarter at the fairground building) and merciful (turning the waystation and first relay stop rather than killing, freeing captives with a genuine choice to flee or fight, taking Vale alive) depending on the moment and who's in front of him — usually erring toward mercy when someone stops actively fighting, but not always. **The defining choice of the campaign so far, at Duskgate:** rather than kill Malphestor, Darkhawk engaged it honestly, learned the anchor (not the devil) was the real target, and risked his own soul to break it instead — leaving Malphestor alive and telling it "if anger can fall, maybe devils can rise." ## Loose Threads

  - **The actual killer:** likely not one person but an organization operating under the name **"Greyfeather"** — three similarly-disguised deaths in Ravenshollow over the past two years, no consistent description across witnesses. Craft-signature evidence now ties Greyfeather to the same maker as the scout creature and the corruption shards. Investigation formally merged with the scout-maker hunt, led by Halvard.
  - **Unexplained:** the order naming Rurik as target came down unusually precise — cage number specified before the match was even scheduled. Corvane himself flagged this as worth taking seriously; unconfirmed why.
  - Still open: Rurik's original opponent's identity and fate — House Corvane's own attempt to trace him went cold decades ago.
  - Still open: how deep House Corvane's service to the Raven Queen runs, and whether it connects to what's above Malphestor. Corvane's own read: his goddess would likely see that thing as a thief in her domain, not a kindred force — unconfirmed, a hopeful theory rather than fact.
- **A possible pattern with the Raven Queen:** Ysolde's working theory is that her faith moves subtly, arranging rather than announcing — and Darkhawk's own Ashpit memory unlocking right before the council, after thirteen years blocked, fits that shape uncomfortably well. Unconfirmed, unprovable, but flagged. **Reads as complementary to "the count" below, not competing with it** — if the Raven Queen genuinely opposes what's above Malphestor, deliberately timing the memory-block's release (rather than the block itself, which the party already suspects came from somewhere else) would be exactly the kind of quiet, indirect intervention her faith favors: helping without announcing it.
- **"The count" — kept strictly between Darkhawk, Mara, Sael, and Ysolde, not yet shared with the wider household.** A dream-borne impression from the bear-spirit the night after the council: something vast and patient has been "counting" Darkhawk since before his memory begins — possibly since the raid that took him as a child, which killed two elders and a hunter and took exactly one child, not a general sweep. Working theory built out together: Ashpit's thirteen years may have been deliberate cultivation rather than cruelty, meant to ensure he'd finish a kill when it mattered; his refusal to do so may be the single most consequential choice of the war so far; Malphestor's broken ledger reflects him becoming genuinely unpredictable rather than just lucky; and the bear-spirit may be actively holding "the count" at zero — a true balance point — with every primal bond the party's formed since potentially part of the same ancient alliance Kethrun described, Darkhawk being prepared to eventually help hold that balance himself rather than just survive it. Deliberately not escalated — Darkhawk chose to prioritize verifiable evidence (Corvane's records, the temple's answer) over pure theory, and to bring this to Vosk, Threnn, Kaldur, and Kethrun directly only once there's something solid to act on, ideally at the Standing Ring where the bond runs strongest. **First real evidence, not just inference (Session 29):** Darkhawk named the theory directly to whatever was watching through the captured constructs' scrying link — the connection flinched hard and cut out immediately. A genuine reaction, not proof of the theory's full shape, but confirmation something is listening and something was rattled by being named. Still kept quiet outside the four, though the bar for "something solid to act on" just moved closer.
- **What's above Malphestor, confirmed to be actively searching for a new foothold** — testing multiple sealed points at once per both Kethrun's and Ilsevet's independent senses of it. Not yet found. Possibly connected to the Raven Queen thread via House Corvane, unconfirmed.
- **The scout creature(s)** — bird-like, "built rather than born," reports back via crude scrying circles, exists in more than one instance. Now formally linked to Greyfeather and pursued as one hunt (see Active Quest Log).
