# World State — Blood Debt

_Living tracker — active threads only. Full history lives in `session-log.md`; this file should always be quick to scan._

## Party
- **Location:** Back at **Urskelde**. All four of Kethrun's known sealed sites are now accounted for. A **full regional council is called for one week out** (from Session 27's date) to decide the Still Throat's guardian question and other matters too large for the party alone. Kaldur remains Commander; Threnn is back from the Drowned Hollow.
- **The Still Throat, resolved with real loss (Session 27).** The fourth and final site — sealed in haste, the worst-off of all four. Its four "guardians" turned out to be its actual original guardians, corrupted into undead **Frostbound Wardens** and destroyed in a hard fight at the pass. The site's true guardian, **Borvik**, survived only as fragments; contact was made, but an overreach trying to connect him to the full network scattered him for good. His last words: the site's own wound **opens down, not up** — different in kind from Kethrun's skyward tear. A black-iron shard matching Urskelde's cairn shard confirms one enemy behind every site, reaching through multiple doors. No guardian remains to hold this site — a group stopgap ward was cast (expected to last days to weeks) rather than leaving it unprotected; a **permanent replacement guardian, freely chosen rather than another sacrifice, is an open question** for the council.
- **Rurik's bond to the sentinel network has deepened into something new.** After serving as conduit for Ilsevet's and Vessyn's reweaving, he found he could reach the thread directly and unassisted — Kethrun confirms this makes him something like a living tending-hand for the network itself, not just a visitor. He can likely sense trouble at connected sites even from a distance. He raised, then chose not to attempt, opening the Still Throat's downward path himself — held as a known option, not used alone.
- **House Corvane's betrayal, resolved (Session 24).** Lord Edrin Corvane publicly confessed to arranging the Ashpit hit as inherited duty to **the Raven Queen**, not personal malice. Offered redemption rather than vengeance, he stayed at Urskelde as a partner (see Key NPCs). The council pledged a full regional alliance against the threat above Malphestor.
- **All four of Kethrun's known sealed sites now accounted for.** Standing Ring, Drowned Hollow, and Sorrow's Hush secured and thread-connected; the Still Throat stopgap-warded after its guardian's loss (see above).
- **The Drowned Hollow, resolved (Session 25).** Found and calmed **Ilsevet**, an ancient goliath-kin who sealed the wound alone in haste centuries ago and had held it by herself ever since, believing herself forgotten. The site was drained via a rediscovered outflow channel, properly reinforced in a two-day ritual (Ysolde, Threnn's ritual-keepers, the bear-spirit anchoring it), and given sustainable tending — **Odra** (one of Threnn's masons) staying on to learn from Ilsevet directly, mirroring Torvald's arrangement with Kethrun.
- **Sorrow's Hush, resolved.** A fundamentally different site — not a wound to an outer threat, but a binding on **Vessyn**, an ancient tree-guardian holding back fear itself rather than an extraplanar tear. Shouting its name (against Bettha's warning) provoked a frightening manifestation; Darkhawk held his ground, then volunteered to channel the sensing-thread reweaving's strain through a deliberately chosen rage rather than risk Vessyn directly. Succeeded — the thread now connects Vessyn, Kethrun, and Ilsevet together. Vessyn marked Darkhawk in turn (see character sheet). No physical repair needed; the site was never damaged, only isolated.
- **The scout threat, active and confirmed real.** A bird-like, "built rather than born" creature — grafted, not fully alive — struck Darkhawk once near the Standing Ring and withdrew rather than press the fight; confirmed via Ysolde's Grave sense and Sael's tracking to be reporting back to something else via crude scrying, and to exist in more than one instance ("building them," not just one scout). A dampening shard was also found planted on one of Urskelde's own cairns — removed, but the backlash cost Ysolde a day's *Sending* and likely alerted whatever placed it.
- **Kaldur formally named Commander of Urskelde** in Darkhawk's absence, full authority to act, announced publicly at the gate. Threnn stands as his political adviser; Halvard's spy network reports to both.
- **The Standing Ring:** hidden stair beneath the central slab leads to **Kethrun**, an ancient goliath-kin-made stone sentinel guarding the original sealed wound. Seal freshly reinforced as of Session 18; Torvald stays on-site to tend it. Urskelde's own boundary cairns are confirmed as the same ancient ward-tradition, "lesser" in scale.
- **The ancient goliath-kin village:** Threnn's people (~3 dozen), released from a centuries-long protective freeze, have fully relocated to and settled at Urskelde.
- **Darkhawk and Mara are married** (Session 16) — see character sheets.
- **Duskgate is fully resolved** — anchor broken, bargain ended, market cleared. **The succession mechanic remains the live security concern:** the bargain needs a living successor to physically reach Duskgate to renew; Vale had groomed **Merrick** for this without telling him. Both are held under complete, tested wards at Urskelde. **Malphestor's fate is unknown** — purposeless, not hostile, hinted at something further up the chain that felt every banked soul vanish.
- **Thundertree losses at the entrance (Session 14):** three warriors dead (Ren Alderhawk, Bram and Torin Stonebrand). Yorik Stonebrand gave Rurik a mourning-stone; Ren's widow Vessa and daughter Kessa asked only that he be remembered as a friend.

## Active leads / evidence
- **Kethrun and the sealed wound** — see Party notes above. Now the primary active thread going into Part Two: directly connects Urskelde's own cairns, the bear-spirit's tradition, and whatever sits above Malphestor searching for a new foothold.
- **Ravenshollow's regional office lead — resolved, low priority.** Halvard traced the unfamiliar signature to Ostrin Vahl, Vale's ordinary bookkeeper, now vanished with no trail; the unidentified third town turned out to be a dead, undeveloped parcel. Halvard's read: someone covering their own tracks, not a hidden conspiracy. Not worth chasing further unless something new surfaces.

## Player Choices of Consequence
A running pattern, not a fixed rule: Darkhawk has moved between ruthless (killing surrendering guards at the mine-road ambush, no-quarter at the fairground building) and merciful (turning the waystation and first relay stop rather than killing, freeing captives with a genuine choice to flee or fight, taking Vale alive) depending on the moment and who's in front of him — usually erring toward mercy when someone stops actively fighting, but not always. **The defining choice of the campaign so far, at Duskgate:** rather than kill Malphestor, Darkhawk engaged it honestly, learned the anchor (not the devil) was the real target, and risked his own soul to break it instead — leaving Malphestor alive and telling it "if anger can fall, maybe devils can rise." Full blow-by-blow of each encounter is in `session-log.md`.
