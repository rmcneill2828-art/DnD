Osric raised whether Kathrivex might have other opposites beyond her; working theory (Rurik, Ysolde, Sael) — balance doesn't require a single counterpart, and other faiths touching choice, mercy, or justice could already be quietly part of it.

- **Osric now in Halvard's charge**, guarded trust; gave up Kathrivex's name as his last honest trade after helping close Greywatch Keep's ledger for good.

## Player Choices of Consequence
A running pattern, not a fixed rule: Darkhawk has moved between ruthless (killing surrendering guards at the mine-road ambush, no-quarter at the fairground building) and merciful (turning the waystation and first relay stop rather than killing, freeing captives with a genuine choice to flee or fight, taking Vale alive) depending on the moment and who's in front of him — usually erring toward mercy when someone stops actively fighting, but not always. **The defining choice of the campaign so far, at Duskgate:** rather than kill Malphestor, Darkhawk engaged it honestly, learned the anchor (not the devil) was the real target, and risked his own soul to break it instead — leaving Malphestor alive and telling it "if anger can fall, maybe devils can rise." Full blow-by-blow of each encounter is in `session-log.md`.

## Loose Threads
- **Who betrayed Darkhawk at Ashpit — resolved (Session 24).** Lord Edrin Corvane confessed publicly: he arranged the hit as inherited family duty to **the Raven Queen**, not personal malice, but never held the blade himself. **Now fully reconciled (Session 32)** — Corvane's remaining sense of owed debt was released after Rurik's audience with the Raven Queen, and he's chosen to stand with the party as kin, freely.
  - **The actual killer:** likely not one person but an organization operating under the name **"Greyfeather"** — three similarly-disguised deaths in Ravenshollow over the past two years, no consistent description across witnesses. Craft-signature evidence now ties Greyfeather to the same maker as the scout creature and the corruption shards. Investigation formally merged with the scout-maker hunt, led by Halvard.
  - **Unexplained:** the order naming Rurik as target came down unusually precise — cage number specified before the match was even scheduled. Corvane himself flagged this as worth taking seriously; unconfirmed why.
  - **Rurik's original opponent — search reopened (Session 40).** House Corvane's ordinary search went cold decades ago, but Osric raised a real new angle: if the count reached the man too, ordinary records wouldn't show it — *ledger* records might. Osric is now cross-referencing the Greywatch Keep archive against the rough timeframe and region. Warned plainly that a name found this way rarely means a happy ending.
- **The Raven Queen, confirmed rather than theorized (Session 32).** Rurik sought her out directly at House Corvane's chantry and named "the count" as theft from her own domain of honestly-settled debts; she confirmed it outright — a genuine counterfeit of her practice, one she cannot act against uninvited but has now been asked to notice. She cannot fight the party's war for them, but marked Rurik with **The Honest Ledger** (see character sheet), a permanent sense for corrupted-choice manipulation. Ysolde's older theory — that timing Rurik's memory-block release before the council was her own quiet, undeclared intervention — reads as consistent with this, though still unconfirmed as to that specific act.
- **"The count" — confirmed real, active, named, theologically reframed, and presented to the full council (Sessions 31–39).** Kethrun confirmed the terminology; Osric gave up the name: **Kathrivex, the First Ledger**, an old, unnamed god opposite the Raven Queen rather than a devil. Darkhawk **fully bonded with the bear-spirit as an equal partner** and has **closed one account outright** — the first closure in Kathrivex's known history. At council, the bear-spirit itself confirmed (speaking directly through Rurik) that its own tradition predates and stands independent of this divine conflict. Still open: why Rurik specifically was marked so precisely before the raid; whether other deities beyond the Raven Queen have taken notice or are already quietly part of the same balance (raised by both Ysolde and Osric); and what Halloway's Reach will reveal.
- **A captive's death, unresolved between Rurik and Ysolde (Session 34).** A fully cooperative, disarmed Greyfeather scout was dropped from a cliff after giving real intelligence freely. Voiced and answered between them, but noted here as part of Rurik's ongoing pattern between mercy and ruthlessness.
- **The scout creature(s)** — bird-like, "built rather than born," reports back via crude scrying circles. Formally linked to Greyfeather and Kathrivex's practice; the Greywatch Keep instances are destroyed, but the wider network presumably continues elsewhere.
