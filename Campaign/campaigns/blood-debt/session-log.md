# Session Log — Blood Debt

## Session 1 — 2026-07-11

Darkhawk Blondin, freshly arrived in the border town of Kessick's Ford, spotted a suspicious "hire notice" on the bounty board — strong backs wanted, no questions asked, pay up front. Intimidated the nervous notice clerk into talking: the posting traced to a mysterious "Vantor Trading Concern," the recruitment was a trap (come alone, unarmed, after dark), and several townsfolk who'd answered similar notices over the past month had never been seen again.

Scouted the Hollowmere estate outside town by daylight, then returned after dark and answered the "invitation" — armed, not alone, and openly skeptical. Talked his way past the gate guards just long enough to provoke a fight, then killed all four in the resulting skirmish (captain and one guard fled on foot; both put down with thrown javelins and a pursuit kill).

Found a boarded outbuilding on the grounds holding six kidnapped townsfolk, including **Tomas Reeve** — the missing merchant's son from another bounty notice. Freed them and sent them to town under Tomas's lead to alert the guard.

Infiltrated the manor itself (tripped a hidden door alarm in the process), ambushed and killed two responding guards in the entry hall, then cornered **Corwin Pell**, the operation's bookkeeper. Under pressure — and after being confronted directly about his complicity — Corwin turned: handed over a trafficking ledger, a sealed letter, payment evidence, guard counts, and the manor's layout, then agreed to turn himself in to Tomas and testify.

Descended to the cellar and cleared it: killed **the Keeper** (the cells' overseer) and three more guards, freed the last two "sorted" captives, and sent them to town as well. Searched the dead and found confirmation that "the Circuit" — the trafficking operation — has standing orders to *report, not engage* anyone connected to "the Ashpit incident," i.e. Darkhawk's own past.

Looted **Vantor's private office**: 280 gold, a hand-drawn regional map (Kessick's Ford, Hollowmere, and **Ravensmoor** named; four more locations marked only with the Circuit's fist-and-chain seal, unidentified), an unsigned letter referencing an unseen superior called **"the Warden"** and a quota for "Ravensmoor numbers before the season opens," and an engraved ceremonial dagger.

Made a deliberate, high-risk choice: piled all ten Hollowmere dead in the service tunnel — Vantor's actual point of entry — with a handwritten note warning him and the Warden that an Ashpit survivor is alive and hunting them. This trades anonymity for a direct psychological strike; expect the Circuit to respond accordingly.

Returned to Kessick's Ford. Tomas and Corwin both safe, the local magistrate now investigating Hollowmere officially (evidence and bodies left in place at Darkhawk's request). Secured Tomas's promise to protect Corwin and his daughter. Took a long rest above the Reeve family's counting house.

**Ends:** fully rested, 3 nights remaining until Vantor is due at Hollowmere.

## Session 2 — 2026-07-11 (continued)

Leveled up (milestone) to **Barbarian 2** — gained Reckless Attack, Danger Sense, and a jump to 27 max HP.

Spent the day in Kessick's Ford tying up loose ends. Told Corwin's daughter her father was a hero; Corwin, visibly moved, committed to testifying fully regardless of cost. Showed Halvard Reeve the recovered Circuit map: he recognized the fist-and-chain seal from a warehouse manifest at **Bramhollow**, a waystation ~2 days out on the Ravensmoor road, and identified it as one of the map's sealed nodes. Struck a standing mutual-aid pact with House Reeve — Halvard relays any Circuit-related intel his trade network picks up, in exchange for Darkhawk's help if House Reeve ever needs it.

Noticed a second sealed node on the map, roughly 3-4 miles **south of Hollowmere**, that Halvard had no explanation for — no manifest, no rumor, nothing his trade network ever touched. He flagged it as more concerning than Bramhollow specifically because it isn't hiding behind legitimate paperwork at all, and pointed toward local trapper **Mara Fenn** as someone who might know the terrain.

Found Mara at the Broken Wheel tavern. She independently confirmed the southern site: a "dead ground" she's avoided for two years (no game, no birds, occasional distant torchlight), and revealed the missing tannery lads used to drink at her regular table — giving her a personal stake. Hired her as a guide (20 gp) to investigate the site, departure set for night, after the swords were ready.

Picked up the reforged longswords from Perrin Dask — properly balanced now for two-weapon fighting, off-hand strikes hit with full Strength behind them instead of the weaker standard off-hand rule.

**Ends:** night has fallen, swords in hand, Mara Fenn ready at the smithy. About to depart for the dead ground south of Hollowmere.

## Session 3 — 2026-07-12

Guided by Mara, infiltrated the **Dead Ground site** south of Hollowmere — a hidden "proving grounds" built into old ruins where captives are forced to fight each other; winners "graduate" onward into the Circuit, losers often don't survive. Ambushed and killed all 6 guards there with Mara's help (bow support throughout). Freed three captives and sent them to Kessick's Ford.

Found a fourth, long-held prisoner: **Kaldur Stonewake**, an older Goliath. He recognized Darkhawk's carried trophy as **Thundertree clan** craftsmanship — clan territory in the high passes, north and east — giving Darkhawk his first real lead on his own identity. Escorted Kaldur back to town for care.

Looted the site: gold, 2 healing potions, a Trial record (11 captives "graduated" this year alone, 17 more blank/unaccounted for), and a duty log confirming guards rotate between Hollowmere, Bramhollow, and this site, and that **Vantor's visit would hit both Hollowmere and this site the same night, right after the Trial closed.**

Leveled up narratively confirmed at Barbarian 2 (Reckless Attack, Danger Sense — noted here for continuity, actually gained just before this session's field work).

Downtime in Kessick's Ford: showed Kaldur the recovered regional map — he confirmed Ravensmoor firsthand (a real market town, Circuit runs beneath it in "season") and flagged a possible second proving-grounds site, "the Western Yard" (unconfirmed, secondhand). Shared his own Ashpit backstory with Kaldur, who offered genuine solidarity — clan isn't only blood. Gathered stakeout supplies (cloaks, muffling wrap) with Mara's guidance. Halvard passed on a warning: someone's already asking around about a large Goliath mercenary, quietly, not via bounty.

**The stakeout:** watched Vantor arrive at Hollowmere's service tunnel with 2 guards, discover the pile of bodies and the note, and panic — overheard him order a rider to Bramhollow to report to **the Warden** personally, confirming Bramhollow is the Warden's direct reporting line, not just a waystation. Raced Mara to the Dead Ground site ahead of Vantor's party and watched him discover the second message pile there too, fully unraveling.

Confronted Vantor directly (unarmed approach, offer to talk instead of kill). It went sideways — his guards panicked, one fled with Vantor toward Bramhollow while the other held the line. Killed the blocking guard, lost the chase. Vantor's own words confirmed **Bramhollow is where he reports to the Warden.**

Pushed Mara for a forced 2-day march to reach Bramhollow ahead of Vantor — succeeded, but the toll caught up (failed CON save, gained Exhaustion 1). Set an ambush outside Bramhollow; concealment was rough (fatigue-driven bad rolls both sides), and the last guard spotted the ambush before it sprang. Fought and killed him with Mara's help.

**Captured Vantor alive**, bound, unharmed. Hid the dead guard's body, retreated to a hidden camp, and took a long rest — exhaustion cleared, full resources restored.

Leveled up (milestone) to **Barbarian 3**, choosing **Path of the Totem Warrior — Bear**: Spirit Seeker (ritual Beast Sense / Speak with Animals) and Totem Spirit granting full damage resistance (except psychic) while raging. HP jumped to 39.

Heading back toward Kessick's Ford, Mara scouted ahead and spotted a 5-rider search party sent from Bramhollow, already hunting for the overdue Vantor. Sent Mara ahead with the bound prisoner while Darkhawk held the road alone. Killed all five riders solo (with Bear Totem's full damage resistance carrying the fight), looted them, botched hiding the bodies (a passing cart likely found the scene), and took their horses to close the distance and catch up with Mara and Vantor.

Rode into Kessick's Ford and delivered Vantor directly to Halvard and Tomas. Under no illusions about his position, **Vantor fully confessed and agreed to turn king's evidence**: confirmed the Warden is a title, not a name he's ever matched to a face — orders arrive only via sealed letters through Bramhollow, never in person. Confirmed the Circuit spans far more territory than what's been seen so far. Admitted he doesn't know who ran Ashpit or who betrayed Darkhawk specifically — only that the incident is used within the Circuit as a cautionary legend. Explained his own entanglement (a debt at 19 he was never allowed to walk away from).

Filled in the last two blanks on the regional map: **the Western Yard** (another proving-grounds site, confirming Kaldur's secondhand rumor) and **Duskgate** (previously the map's last unknown mark — not a fighting site at all, but a private buyer market east of Ravensmoor where the highest-value "graduates" are actually sold). The map is now fully identified.

Handed Vantor to Tomas for the magistrate, with an explicit message: if the law doesn't deliver justice with what Vantor and Corwin have both given them, Darkhawk will be the justice instead. Closed the day out on a quiet, reflective exchange with Mara about law versus justice.

**Ends:** Vantor in custody and cooperating. Hollowmere, the Dead Ground, and the Bramhollow road are all resolved. Open threads for next time: the Warden's true identity, Bramhollow itself (still not directly investigated), the Western Yard, Duskgate, Ravensmoor/the Grand Circuit, and Thundertree clan.

Noted for the next milestone level-up: player wants to multiclass 1 level into Fighter (Second Wind + a Fighting Style, to be picked together when it happens).

**Ends:** resting in Kessick's Ford, one day out from the Bramhollow job. Plan is set: quiet infiltration, Mara solo on the office, Darkhawk + Joss on overwatch, 30-minute trigger.

## Session 5 — 2026-07-14

**Executed the Bramhollow job successfully and quietly.** Joss held outside lookout, Mara infiltrated the warehouse office alone within the 30-minute window and extracted ledgers plus two letters in the Warden's own sealed hand — real physical proof the Warden exists beyond Vantor's word. Darkhawk silently killed and hid one off-pattern patrol guard who nearly discovered the group; no alarm raised, no bodies left visible, no declaration. Joss's lookout contract concluded successfully.

Reviewed all recovered evidence together and made a key deduction: **"the Circuit" implies a closed loop, but every confirmed site (Hollowmere, Dead Ground, Western Yard, Bramhollow, Ravensmoor, Duskgate) is a satellite — intake, processing, distribution, or sale. None of them is the actual hub the Warden operates from, and Ashpit's location is never once stated in any recovered document.** Took this theory to Halvard (now quietly tracing Circuit money) and Magistrate Kell (now checking property/land records) — both agreed to investigate in parallel.

A tavern rumor, followed up in person, independently pointed the same direction: unusual land purchases (old mine workings, paid well above value, buyer untraceable, via a broker firm called **Ravenshollow** out of the regional seat) in the foothills of **the Greyhorn Range — Thundertree's home territory.** Mountain clan trade contacts confirm the Goliath clans there have grown noticeably more closed-off over roughly the same period. Strong circumstantial case that the Circuit's true center sits right where Darkhawk's own lost clan lives.

Arranged **Fallow Cross** as a forward operating base via Halvard — a House Reeve trade post between the Western Yard and the road toward Ravensmoor/Duskgate, staffed by warehouse keeper **Ressa Dane**, with courier **Pell Ashworth** running messages and evidence back to Kessick's Ford on a schedule.

Told Kaldur about the Greyhorn lead; he agreed to guide Darkhawk into the mountains once the more immediate Circuit threats are dealt with — his own clan (Ninebells) is different from Thundertree, but he knows how to move in that country without provoking a watchful clan.

**Leveled up (milestone, for completing Bramhollow):** multiclassed into **Fighter 1** — Second Wind, and the **Protection** fighting style (reaction: impose disadvantage on an attack against an ally within 5 ft while wielding a shield — gives the back-harness shield real tactical purpose). Rolled poorly for HP (+3), bringing max HP to 42.

Also picked up a steel shield with a quick-release back harness from Perrin and a rebalanced ceremonial dagger, and had **sky-iron** (rare meteoric ore) worked into both longswords — not magical, but the blades now count as magical weapons for overcoming resistance/immunity to nonmagical attacks.

**Hit the Western Yard as planned — loud and deliberate this time, by design.** Walked in visibly rather than sneaking, backed by Mara sniping from a ridge. Declared the kill count aloud before the fight (a calculated psychological strike) and fought openly, killing all 6 guards. Took the officer alive as intended, then delivered an explicit, personal message rather than another anonymous note: **"I am Darkhawk Blondin, left for dead by the Warden... 31 dead, only you left alive... there is a storm coming."** Released him to carry it back to Bramhollow. This was a deliberate strategic shift, reasoned through carefully beforehand: an unnamed "Ashpit ghost" rumor is more dangerous to Kessick's Ford than a named, specific target is, since retaliation with no clear target tends to land on the nearest soft target instead. Freed 2 more captives from the site's cells and sent them to Tomas as well.

Secured a small amount of official backing from Magistrate Kell before the operation: he can't send soldiers, but formally recorded that any Circuit retaliation should be understood as targeting Darkhawk specifically, not the town, and raised his own guardsmen's alertness.

**Ends:** at Fallow Cross, Bramhollow evidence and Western Yard both resolved. Darkhawk's identity is now openly known to the Circuit — no longer a rumor. Open threads: Duskgate (personally tied to Ashpit), Ravensmoor, the Greyhorn foothills/Ravenshollow/Thundertree hub lead, and the ongoing Halvard/Kell investigations.

## Session 6 — 2026-07-14/15

Planned the next move at Fallow Cross over breakfast: deliberately deferred Duskgate (too well-defended for two people right now) and the Greyhorn foothills (Kaldur not ready, lead still circumstantial), choosing to scout Ravensmoor first while it's still an "ordinary" town, before the first-frost season starts. Traveled there posing as **"Mr. and Mrs. Blondin,"** a couple looking to open a smithy and a provisioning business, deliberately keeping the cover close to real skills (Darkhawk's real time at Perrin's forge, Mara's real hunting/provisioning background) and moving at an unhurried, unsuspicious pace.

In Ravensmoor: cleared the gate easily, checked into the Hound's Rest, and got two names unprompted from separate locals — **Master Corlin Vey** (the town's land broker) and confirmation that land near the fairgrounds is priced strangely high for a town its size. A stranger in the tavern took real interest in the couple; a fake-drunk bait attempt (badly botched) spooked him into leaving fast and clean, and Mara lost his trail entirely — someone genuinely skilled at not being followed.

Met Vey in person: legitimate broker, but visibly uneasy about the fairground district specifically — a separate, unnamed "holding company" manages those buildings and he's learned not to ask questions. Scouting the district directly, spotted one building that doesn't match the "unused off-season" story (reinforced door, fresh wagon ruts, a recently-used candle-wax watch post) and a permanent watcher stationed near Vey's own office.

**Acquired Old Harlan's Forge** — a real, legitimate property at the edge of the fairground district, leased through Vey — giving Darkhawk and Mara a permanent, unsuspicious foothold to watch the suspicious building long-term. Discovered a well-maintained, deliberately unobtrusive **root-cellar hatch** around back of that building, separate from its main door.

Began rotating night watches on the hatch. Mara witnessed one man use it casually around midnight. Darkhawk later witnessed a muffled wagon delivering crates and an **empty locked cage** to the building in the dead of night — hard evidence that "unused off-season" is false, and that whatever this site does isn't limited to the twice-yearly fair.

**Major breakthrough — pieced together the Circuit's full structure:** intake sites (Hollowmere) feed proving grounds (Dead Ground, Western Yard) which cull and test captives; survivors get shipped to Ravensmoor, where "season" is itself another proving ground in front of buyers and promoters; the actual best of the best don't stay in the public circuit at all — they're sent somewhere private, whispered about like a legend rather than a location. **Ashpit isn't closed history. It's the ongoing, hidden top of the entire pyramid** — which may make it the same place as the Circuit's missing "true center" the Greyhorn foothills lead already pointed toward.

This realization triggered real, dangerous anger — the instinct to storm the hatch immediately. Held to the "no more surprises" pact instead: told Mara directly, let the anger be felt without acting on it (with her steady support), and returned to the plan.

While keeping watch alone that night, deliberately reached for suppressed childhood memory for the first time — recovered real, vivid fragments: cold mountain air, woodsmoke, a voice singing in Giant, the trophy given to him with ceremony, and the fractured moment of being taken, his own name called out behind him — audible in shape and rhythm, but the actual sound still wouldn't resolve. Confirmed to himself: finishing that memory requires standing in front of Thundertree in person.

Shared the memory fully with Mara. The relationship shifted from professional partnership to openly mutual and romantic — a kiss during the cover performance "came out sounding a little too real" for both of them, and they explicitly acknowledged it wasn't an act. Darkhawk asked Mara to be with him when he finally faces Thundertree; she committed without hesitation.

**Leveled up (milestone, belated credit for the Western Yard's full clear — a full fight plus the public identity reveal that was never actually credited before this):** **Fighter 2**, gaining **Action Surge** (once per rest, an additional action — doubles a turn's dual-wield attacks). Rolled poorly for HP again (+4), bringing max HP to 46.

**Ends:** at Old Harlan's Forge, Ravensmoor, mid-investigation. The hatch is being watched nightly. Duskgate and the Greyhorn foothills/Ashpit both loom as the next major destinations, with a strong working theory that they may be connected or even identical.

**Major unprompted lead:** Greta revealed she's unknowingly been supplying cage-grade hardware (shackle stock, hinges, two heavy sliding bolts) to an anonymous buyer for roughly two years — paid in cash via a silent ~12-year-old courier, orders scaling up over time. Bigger payoff: the anonymous buyer's next hardware order is due within the week, and Greta will let Darkhawk carry that delivery himself instead of her usual quiet handoff to the boy — a legitimate reason to walk goods toward wherever the courier's chain actually leads, possibly the building itself.

Met back up with Mara, who'd finished scouting the town hall records office solo: one lightly-attentive night watchman on a ~40-minute circuit, a cheap alley-side door the day clerks use. Agreed to split the work — Mara handles the town hall break-in that night (deed only, no unnecessary risk), Darkhawk returns to Greta the next day for the breastplate fitting and apprentice walkthrough, and both start real repair work on Old Harlan's Forge to keep the cover solid.

Spent the afternoon on genuine forge repairs (cracked bellows, rotted tool pegs, a dead hinge) — real work, not just cover dressing. That evening, ran the town hall job Bramhollow-style: Mara alone inside, Darkhawk on nearby backup (no outside lookout this time), a 30-minute trigger, and a taught owl-call signal (two soft hoots = clear, one sharp cry = trouble). Darkhawk kept watch with advantage on his Perception check (rolled 12, taking the higher of two d20s) and caught the "clear" signal on schedule. Mara emerged clean with the ownership ledger entry copied by hand.

**Major discovery:** the Ravensmoor fairground building's deed is held by **Ravenshollow** — the exact same broker firm quietly buying up old mine workings in the Greyhorn foothills near Thundertree territory. Same name behind both a "processing" site in Ravensmoor and unexplained land purchases in Darkhawk's ancestral mountains — strong evidence Ravenshollow isn't a front the Circuit rents, but a company the Warden's network actually owns.

Wrote the finding up that night and paid a discreet local rider to carry it to **Ressa Dane at Fallow Cross** for onward relay via Pell Ashworth to Halvard and Kell in Kessick's Ford — judged too important to sit on until the courier-delivery lead plays out.

Closed the night at Old Harlan's Forge — first full, unwatched rest either of them has had in some time, and the relationship with Mara deepened further, openly sharing the night together.

**Ends:** fully rested at Old Harlan's Forge, Ravensmoor, morning of the next day. Breastplate fitting and apprentice walkthrough with Greta scheduled for today. Open threads: the courier boy's next delivery (within the week — Darkhawk's way toward the building itself), the Ravenshollow connection now in transit to Halvard/Kell, the courier boy's eventual safety, Duskgate, and the Greyhorn foothills/Thundertree.

A careful group examination (Ysolde, Sael, Wren) confirmed it as a genuine network convergence point — a dozen-plus distinct threads, some clearly construct-linked, some reading as live and possibly human, two seemingly running toward the other priority sites (the coastal village, the burned court). Vosk's hunters barricaded the main gate and both side entrances from the inside, securing the ground floor. A floor search turned up real evidence of who's actually behind the network's infrastructure: a records room too dense to parse quickly (flagged for Halvard), a study with a half-finished letter referencing an "R." who treats even Kathrivex as a resource rather than a master ("*the eye sees what the count cannot... R. is patient, but not infinitely so*"), and personal quarters holding a child's toy, a hairpin, and a degraded letter ending "*come home when the count is settled, my love*" — the first real proof that whoever runs this isn't hollowed out, has people they love, and is very much a person rather than an abstraction.

Ysolde Sent word to Halvard directly: the keep is occupied, a hub found, "R." traced, reinforcement and his archive needed urgently. His reply came back fast — leaving within the hour. Vosk dispatched a hunter running light and fast toward Kaldur with the same news, expected to arrive within two days.

**Ends here for now:** the mountain keep breached, secured rather than destroyed, and confirmed as a genuine network hub — the clearest proof yet that something above Kathrivex's own count is running this operation, with a name at last: **"R."** Halvard incoming with his archive, Kaldur being alerted by runner, and the party holding occupied ground deep in territory nobody in this alliance had ever mapped before today.

**Down, and Elenna.** Rather than wait on reinforcement, the party took the unexplored third path from the entry hall — down, into the mountain rather than up or further in. A row of long-disused cells gave way to one occupied: a living woman in magically-induced stasis, malnourished but stable. Ysolde brought her back gently. She gave her name as **Elenna** — wife of **Aldren Voss**, a once-honest trader who took on a bad debt years ago and was slowly drawn deeper into Kathrivex's network as payment on a debt that never actually closed, eventually stationed here. A comb pendant at her throat matched the hairpin found in the study upstairs, confirming it was her own room the party had searched. Rurik told her the truth of everything — the pit, the count, every account closed since, direct contact with Kathrivex itself — and she chose to believe it, offering everything she knows in return once Halvard arrives.

She confirmed real, useful detail on "R.": met twice, calm and patient, treats Kathrivex as a tool rather than a master, and spoke once of "scale" bigger than even this keep or the count itself — something still being built toward. She also placed her own hometown as a poor fishing town south along the coast from Halloway's Reach, an uncomfortably close match to the alliance's second priority site.

Continuing past her cell, the party found a message scratched into an empty cell's wall, recent and deliberate: **"A. VOSS — MOVED NORTH, ALIVE AS OF THIS WRITING. THEY DON'T KILL THE USEFUL ONES. ASK FOR THE BURNED COURT."** A locked storage room yielded a cruder transfer ledger tracking people and constructs moved between sites — confirming the burned court and a coastal site matching Elenna's description, plus a wholly new fourth location never before named: **"the Reckoning House."** A forced-open chest turned up an unidentified enchanted silver ring (protective in nature, per Ysolde's Arcana read — no one in the party can cast Identify, so it's logged for a real enchanter later) alongside minor personal effects taken from past captives. Deepest in, a round chamber held a second **black scrying mirror**, the same design as the one recovered years ago from the Warden's man — likely this keep's own line to R. or beyond. A short contact log beside it shows recent use. Untouched and untested as the exploration paused — the party debating whether to risk it or wait for Halvard.

**The workshop, and three more freed.** The last unexplored path from the entry hall led "deeper in" to a full construct-manufacturing workshop — a dozen-plus coerced human workers laboring under two armed overseers, exactly the kind of forced labor Elenna's account had already implied. Rurik walked in alone, party stealthed as backup, and declared himself the keep's new authority outright. An intimidation attempt to force a bloodless surrender failed, and the fight that followed was short and one-sided: Mara's opening volley (Hunter's Mark plus Dread Ambusher) dropped the first overseer in a single turn; the second, watching that happen, surrendered rather than fight on. He gave his name as **Corbett** — a former caravan guard trapped by the same debt-and-rescue trick that's caught nearly everyone else this campaign has freed, two years into service, recently promoted to overseer against his own comfort with it. Two more guards, **Fenwick** and **Tamsin**, answered a shift-bell summons and surrendered without a fight once they saw the numbers arrayed against them, each with their own debt story — poaching fines sold on, an inherited family debt Tamsin volunteered into believing it would clear her whole family's name.

All three confirmed the workshop answers to a **Keeper Ilsa**, absent three days on an errand toward the coast, which explains the keep's thin security. Rurik gave the freed workers and all three former guards the same choice he's offered before: go home once Halvard identifies where that is, or stay and work to actively close accounts instead of opening them. Fenwick chose home; Tamsin asked to decide once she knows if she still has a home to go to; Corbett, carrying real guilt over his two years of complicity, chose to stay and work it off doing something that matters.

**Three accounts closed, and a hub that noticed.** Concentrating on their threads directly, Rurik confirmed all three guards were still actively counted, uncut. He severed all three at once — clean, same as every prior closing — but this time, standing inside the hub's own reach rather than out at its edges, the crystal flared hard and visibly rather than rippling quietly. Ysolde confirmed something felt it directly, close, for the first time. Given the risk, the party held off severing the dozen-plus freed workers' own threads in the same room, choosing physical freedom and Halvard's help home over further disturbing the hub blind.

**Full sweep.** A careful reassessment of the hub (advantage, group effort) confirmed it stable afterward, the three severed threads visibly dark and clean, no active response detected. A second pass at the workshop (properly done with Help this time, after an initial rushed look turned up nothing) revealed the hub crystal is structurally *necessary* to finish any unfinished construct — meaning severing it would leave every incomplete frame in this keep permanently inert — plus a real materials/supply list worth handing to Halvard or Perrin and Greta. A hidden floor compartment yielded Keeper Ilsa's private journal, more detailed than the transfer ledger, giving rough directions toward **the Reckoning House** rather than just the name. Wren tested the compass by speaking that name aloud directly to it — it reacted, confirming the name means something real, but couldn't yet settle on a clean bearing.

A last storage alcove held the session's most unsettling find: an unfinished, more refined construct — notes describing "assessment capacity" and "count-integrity verification," reading like early design work for something in the Auditor's own family, more advanced. A second unidentified initial signed off on its development: **"K."**, distinct from R. Both side entrances checked clean — the west a plain delivery route, the east a maintenance corridor whose fresh wagon and boot tracks confirmed Keeper Ilsa's own three-day-old departure, heading the same direction as Aldren — toward the burned court.

**The keep, fully explored.** Every room, passage, and lead now accounted for or flagged for Halvard's incoming archive work: the hub, the records room, the study, the quarters, the entry hall, the workshop, the cell block, the storage room, the scrying chamber, both side entrances, and the storage-level alcoves.

**Taking stock.** Corbett gave a rough accounting of the keep's provisions — three to four weeks of food for the current reduced headcount, a reliable internal spring for water, thin medical supplies, a modest coin store, and the workshop's own crystal/material stock as the real point of value. Rurik tasked him with a full written inventory ahead of Halvard's arrival, Fenwick assisting — the first real, freely-chosen work either of them has done since surrendering.

**Halvard arrives.** Three days later, Halvard rode in alone, archive satchel heavy, exactly as promised. Rurik walked him through the entire keep in the order it was discovered, nothing disturbed beforehand. Standout reactions: the construct ranks confirmed the same craft signature as the corrupted den and Greyfeather's makers, tying the investigations together further; the hub crystal he examined only from a respectful distance, trusting the party's own read; the records room took him nearly two hours and turned up a shipping pattern matching Osric's ship-registry anomaly, proof the keep's reach extends to fronts the alliance hasn't identified yet; the "R." letter he called the single most valuable piece of paper recovered this whole campaign, and had copied multiple times before touching the original further; the prototype alcove and "K." signature left him visibly grave, with no immediate match in his own records but real leads through his Kessick's Ford contacts to chase once home.

He then took full, careful accounts from **Elenna** (more detail on Aldren and R., a direct promise to search for him properly, "not a courtesy") and **Corbett** (operational knowledge of Ilsa's habits and the keep's rhythms, including the detail that she left on fast horses in a tense final week — likely pressure from above rather than routine). **Fenwick and Tamsin** added smaller pieces, Tamsin's most valuable: an overheard fragment that "the Reckoning House keeps its own count, separate from the rest" — possible evidence of a partially independent operation. Finally, Halvard spent hours with the dozen-plus freed workshop workers, logging every name and hometown and promising staggered, escorted travel home, word sent ahead where he has contacts (the Kessick's Ford man wept openly hearing his family would know he was coming before he arrived).

**Halvard's assessment.** Asked plainly what he thought, he named it clearly: the alliance walked into this campaign believing it fought a god's own network, and today confirmed it's fighting *people* who use that network as a leveraged resource — potentially more dangerous for having patterns and limits that don't follow any god's. Two names, R. and K., at minimum two hands on the wheel. He recommended treating the burned court as urgent rather than merely "third priority," since it's now where both Aldren and Ilsa are believed to have gone — likely toward R. or someone close to R. directly.

**The decision to move fast.** Rather than wait for a full council briefing, Rurik chose to push straight for the burned court and delegated the complete reporting duty to Halvard — everything from the Session 43 council through everything found today, compiled properly rather than piecemeal. Two concerns were raised and addressed: the hub crystal (Halvard's recommendation: a real rotating garrison once word reaches Kaldur, same shape as the sealed-site tending arrangements, not indefinite reliance on the expedition's own hunters) and the alliance's manpower being stretched thinner than ever across seven-plus live fronts now (acknowledged plainly, no easy fix beyond the already-authorized recruitment campaign and careful pacing).

**Corbett placed in charge of the keep.** Rurik's own solution to the garrison question: leave Corbett — who knows the keep better than anyone available — in day-to-day charge as a chance to prove himself, with Vosk's hunters patrolling the perimeter and reporting independently back to the council, the same guarded-trust structure already proven with Voss at Halloway's Reach. Corbett accepted it seriously, asking honestly why he'd been chosen before committing fully. Vosk set the patrol rotation without hesitation.

**Ends here for now:** the mountain keep fully secured, explored, and handed to a real (if guarded) local authority; Halvard finishing his archive work and departing soon after with the complete report for the full council; and the party, resting one final night at the keep, set to push at first light straight toward the burned court — no return to Urskelde first, the freshest lead on Aldren, Ilsa, and possibly R. themself not given time to go cold.

**On the road again.** The column set out southeast at first light, genuinely unmapped ground from here. An hour in, the first Tier 3 travel roll triggered: Sarna found a stretch of ground scorched in small, deliberate, non-natural patches — possibly tied to the destination's name — and Kell found a second, newer set of tracks (six or seven strong) crossing Keeper Ilsa's own trail roughly a day behind her. Rurik's count-sense read the newer tracks as count-tainted.

**The burned patches.** A close examination (Ysolde and Wren, advantage) revealed the scorch marks weren't damage at all — a deliberate trail of ritual "closing" burns, each one containing fragments of destroyed ledger pages. Surviving text confirmed the practice: debts "satisfied by fire," accounts closed by erasing the paper trail rather than freeing the person the debt was ever attached to — the dark mirror of Rurik's own pattern. The burn trail marked a clear route continuing toward the burned court.

**Tracking the seven.** Sarna and Kell worked out the newer trail properly: seven individuals, trained gait, one notably lighter-framed, moving parallel to Ilsa's route rather than following it directly, roughly a day to a day and a half back. Judging it worth the cost, Rurik pushed the pace hard for two days running (a self-imposed +2 penalty to the Tier 3 travel trigger for reduced caution, per the new risk-tier tools) — the first day clean, closing the gap to half a day; the second day rolled a natural 1 on the travel check, and the pushed pace itself caused the encounter: cresting a ridge, the party ran headlong into the group with no warning on either side. Sael scouted ahead as a hawk the evening before and spotted distant smoke, likely their camp, confirming the direction.

**The standoff.** Mutual surprise, weapons undrawn at first. The group's leader — hooded, calm, clearly in command — offered to talk rather than fight, correctly guessing Rurik's reputation ("the anti-count") had reached this far. A tense, genuinely searching conversation followed: the leader admitted working for whoever pays them **by choice**, not debt, and pushed back hard when Rurik challenged that as an easy option — revealing they'd watched someone they loved die taking the "principled" road once, and chose this path specifically to avoid that happening again. Rurik answered with his own raw honesty about the daily weight of real choices, which visibly landed and genuinely shifted the tone. The leader conceded ground, refused to name their employer, but confirmed the group was on a separate delivery errand toward the burned court, not hunting the party. Rurik drew his weapon anyway, judging the vague answer wasn't enough — the leader called it fairly ("you just made the choice for me") before combat began.

**The fight.** Sael opened with Spike Growth centered on the whole group; the leader Misty Stepped clear immediately, abandoning their own soldiers to the terrain. Mara opened with Hunter's Mark and (this being her true first turn of the new engagement) Dread Ambusher's third attack, hitting the leader hard. Rurik raged and closed the distance. The soldiers, still boxed in the spikes, chose to push through rather than hold — collectively took over 100 damage from the terrain alone before ever landing a blow, wounding two hits on Sael, Ysolde, and Wren in return. Wren's Chain Lightning, aimed squarely at the already-wounded soldiers rather than the leader, killed three outright and left a fourth on 2 HP. Ysolde's Bless carried the party's accuracy through the rest of it. The leader, watching their people die in an instant, tried to flee — Rurik's opportunity attack caught them mid-turn. Mara finished the 2-HP soldier and wounded another. Sael's Moonbeam (replacing Spike Growth, since both are concentration) burned two more soldiers badly on their following turn. Rurik closed it out exactly as ordered — two more hits, the second reducing the leader to 0 HP, **knocked unconscious rather than killed**, per his explicit intent. The three surviving soldiers surrendered without further prompting, watching their leader go down and half their number already dead.

**Result:** the Hooded Leader captured alive, unconscious and stable. Three soldiers restrained and compliant. Four soldiers dead. Not one party member seriously hurt the entire fight.

**The interrogation.** Ysolde revived the Leader with a touch of Cure Wounds. Rurik turned the three surviving soldiers to face them directly, naming plainly that their leader had abandoned them in the spikes and nearly fled outright — the soldiers, hearing it said aloud, openly agreed rather than defended her. Offered plain freedom (one sword, one free hand, no strings) rather than a debt or a job, two soldiers stayed uncertain while a third — asked directly why he'd extend mercy to the woman who'd have let him die — gave a genuinely thoughtful answer: he didn't want revenge, handed to him as a "gift," to be the first free choice he ever made on his own terms. Rurik freed him on the spot. The other two stayed restrained for the return to the keep. Offered a harsher choice — fight her own remaining soldiers for freedom, one sword, or take a different path — the Leader broke fully, and Rurik pulled the offer back once the soldiers themselves made clear they didn't want to be handed vengeance either.

A thorough search of the four dead soldiers turned up a hidden token sewn into a coat lining: a fist gripping unbalanced scales, the first physical symbol ever tied to **"K."**, distinct from R.'s eye-and-broken-chain mark — strong evidence K. runs an independent operation paying its own tribute to the burned court.

**The retreat.** Weighing the new intelligence — a disguised meeting point, an unopened case of unknown danger, and confirmation the burned court commands enough respect to be paid tribute rather than simply obeyed — Rurik called off the direct push and turned the column back toward the mountain keep instead, judging it wiser to consolidate everything found with Halvard before committing further. The party departs with the Hooded Leader and two soldiers restrained, the newly-freed soldier traveling along undecided, the recovered crates, and the K. token.

**A complication on the road home.** A day into the Tier 2 return leg, the locked case began emitting a faint, regular ticking sound it hadn't made before — unexplained, growing marginally louder. Even the Leader, genuinely alarmed, confirmed it's new behavior. A careful joint examination (Ysolde and Wren) identified it as a schedule-based failsafe ward tied to the delivery route and timing, now counting down since the column diverted from its expected path — likely rigged to alert the intended recipients and/or destroy the contents once triggered, estimated hours to a day out. A tense, hands-on disarming (two close calls where the countdown briefly sped up before being caught) unwound the ward fully rather than triggering it. Rurik chose to hold the case unopened until back at the keep rather than risk it further on the road.

**Home to the keep.** The rest of the journey passed clean. Corbett managed the return arrival smoothly — prisoners separated (Leader alone, the two soldiers together, to limit coordinated stories), cargo unloaded and the case set apart untouched, exactly the kind of competent handling that's continuing to earn him the trust he was given.

**The case, opened — and a devastating realization.** With Halvard present, Rurik opened the case himself. Inside: roughly a dozen small crystal shards, each one humming with the same wrongness as an individual person's severed thread rather than construct-craft — Ysolde's read, unhesitating: these are fragments of actual human accounts, crystallized and preserved. Not cargo. **Tribute paid in literal pieces of people**, currency between R.'s organization and the burned court.

Rurik made the connection that reframed the entire campaign's central mystery: devils deal in souls, not debts — so if Malphestor, "the Reckoner," was ever genuinely bound to serve the Warden's bargain, it was never truly working for Kathrivex's ledger at all. It was doing exactly what devils always do, using soul-currency the debt framing had been quietly built on top of and around from the start. Rurik pushed the implication one step further: this is very likely why no other power — the Nine Hells specifically — has ever moved against Kathrivex's count, despite its scale. They're not blind to it. They're profiting from it, paid in real souls regardless of whose ledger the transaction runs through. Halvard and Ysolde both confirmed the logic independently, Ysolde noting it directly answers the "have other deities noticed" question raised and left open at the Session 43 council: **yes, and at least one of them is being paid to look away.**

Halvard named Malphestor an urgent thread again rather than an old loose end — the one witness who might know firsthand exactly how deep the Nine Hells' involvement actually runs, and whether it's a wall this alliance can never break, or simply an arrangement that could be disrupted the same way everything else has been.

**One more implication.** Rurik pushed the soul-currency theory further still: if the shards are genuine, and the hub crystal already converts raw material into finished constructs, there's no clear reason the same hub couldn't be crystallizing soul-fragments from the live threads running through it too — meaning the keep might still be an active soul-shard foundry sitting a few floors above where the party stood, not just a construct one. Unconfirmed, but grim enough to reopen the still-unresolved question of what to actually do with the hub.

**The mirror, used.** Rather than sit on the uncertainty, Rurik chose to activate the untested scrying mirror directly — deliberately, with a clear goal: learn how important this site actually is to whoever answers, since that answer would decide whether risking live threads to sever the hub is the right call at all. Wren and Ysolde worked out the activation cleanly. The contact answered expecting a routine check-in from Keeper Ilsa and got Rurik instead.

What followed was a real negotiation. Rurik declared his full identity and reputation outright — Darkhawk, Cage 7, the anti-count, breaker of the anchor — and the contact, a mid-level coordinator who gave his name as **Corren**, visibly recognized every piece of it, admitting the rumors reaching the network were apparently true. Pressed plainly, Corren confirmed the keep genuinely matters to people above him and tried to trade information (real coordinates on the coastal town, more on "the Reckoning House," possibly Aldren and Ilsa's whereabouts) for time. Rurik rejected the trade, called out the mismatch in negotiating skill, demanded contact with someone senior enough to actually speak for R. or K., and closed with a pointed warning: Wren's Fireball is still unused. Corren, rattled but professional, agreed to escalate the request rather than promise anything himself — no timeline given, no guarantee R. or K. would respond at all, but a real channel now genuinely open for the first time.

**Ends here for now:** the mountain keep's true stakes laid bare — a possible active soul-shard foundry as well as a construct one — and, for the first time in this entire campaign, a direct, acknowledged line of contact opened with the network above Kathrivex itself, awaiting whoever Corren manages to reach.

**A plan for what comes next.** Rurik laid out five clear steps: Halvard finishes fully questioning the prisoners, then finalizes his report; Vosk's hunters, Halvard, and the escort prisoners head back to Urskelde; Halvard briefs the full council and opens a real search for Malphestor; and the party itself holds the mountain keep directly, securing every entrance, while awaiting Corren's response — accepting plainly that the hub crystal may not be salvageable if the negotiation fails.
