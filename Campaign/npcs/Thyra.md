# Thyra

**Goliath Fighter (Battle Master) 9** — Darkhawk's sister, Deputy Commander of Urskelde, named Rurik's successor (Session 51). Publicly affirmed and field-tested at Greywatch Keep (Session 52), where she led a wolf pack defense that killed all four attackers without a single injury to the crew under her watch.

## Features
- **Action Surge** (Fighter 2): once per short/long rest, take one additional action on her turn.
- **Second Wind** (Fighter 1): bonus action, once per short/long rest, regain 1d10+9 HP.
- **Battle Master maneuvers** (4 superiority dice, d8): **Disarming Attack** (on a hit, spend a die to add it to damage and force a Strength save or drop a held item) and **Trip Attack** (on a hit, spend a die to add it to damage and force a Strength save or fall prone) — the same two maneuvers she and Rurik traded in sparring (Session 34).
- **Powerful Build** (goliath): counts as one size larger for carrying capacity and push/drag/lift.
- **Stone's Endurance** (goliath): reaction, once per short/long rest, reduce damage taken by 1d12 + CON modifier (+3).
- **Mountain Born** (goliath): acclimated to high altitude and cold climates.

## Equipment
- **Sky-iron set** (Session 50, Perrin & Greta): breastplate, vambraces, greaves, open-faced helm. Lighter joints, fitted and drilled specifically for her.
- Spear, standard adventuring gear, Deputy Commander's signet.

## Background & Notes
Darkhawk's younger sister, training under Kaldur since his return to Urskelde. Held her own in a clean sparring rematch against Rurik (Session 34) after her own public trial. Named Rurik's successor for the Urskelde/Greywatch Keep contingency plan (Session 51) — if he doesn't return from the Reckoning House, she takes over what he's building and falls back to Greywatch Keep with Sesha and Harek.

**Session 52 field test:** ran the Greywatch Keep expedition's day-to-day directly at Rurik's insistence ("don't seek my approval"), affirmed by Threnn, Perrin, Joss, and Corim. Handled a day of disruptions (staged and real) well, caught the pattern herself, and led the defense against a wolf pack that broke through an unwatched wall breach — no injuries to anyone under her protection, but she immediately identified the watch gap as her own oversight rather than luck, and set a real muster/watch plan in motion afterward. Still figuring out where "Deputy Commander" ends and "little sister" begins — Rurik's told her plainly she doesn't have to choose.
