**Previously:** Session 52 — 2026-08-28 (**Greywatch Keep: fortification fixed, Thyra fully tested.**) The keep's real weak point — an unfinished eastern breach, watched by nobody in particular — let a wolf pack through on day 3; killed cleanly, zero injuries, but it exposed exactly the gap Thyra needed to find in herself. Over the following days: the breach sealed solo by Rurik (now the strongest stretch of wall), the west wall surveyed clean (a goliath-kin boundary marker found and left standing), a real sky-iron gate started for the doorless old gatehouse, and a proper watch rotation and waste-disposal discipline both made standing practice. Rurik pushed Thyra through a real gauntlet of leadership lessons — owning the watch-gap herself rather than resting on a clean win, learning to ask for help instead of soloing a problem, holding a genuine "no" against a direct order (even reframed as one), and being told plainly that the whole trip's real point was her, not the ore. The capstone: Rurik and Mara told her (truthfully, incompletely) they were checking on something sensed through the count, then secretly watched Greywatch Keep for a full day and night from a ridge, close enough to intervene, while Thyra ran full independent command in her own style — competently, and at real emotional cost once the absence stretched into worry she never let stop her from working. The reunion closed the one true gap left in the succession plan: Ysolde's Sending spell (plus more Sending Stones to come) for regular check-ins, a week/month escalation to full succession, and a standing rule — grieve in private, command in public.

**Last session:** Session 53 — 2026-09-01 (**Kathrivex, the First Ledger, destroyed — the count closed for good.**) A quiet, joyful six-week stretch at Urskelde (Darkhawk actually resting for the first time in the whole campaign, a real bear's silent approval on a walk in the woods, a running "who's the boss" bit with Mara) gave way to the campaign's climax: the avatar armor finished (switched to half-plate mid-fitting to preserve Fast Movement, AC 21), a teleportation circle went live between Urskelde and Greywatch Keep, and Halvard's surveillance finally located the Reckoning House. A five-person strike team (Darkhawk, Mara, Sael, Ysolde, Wren) captured and humanely interrogated a Greyfeather patrol, bluffed past both compound checkpoints, destroyed two anchor-bound guardians, then Darkhawk reached the true ledger-anchor directly and provoked Kathrivex into a full avatar manifestation. **Killed in three rounds** — Wren's long-held Meteor Swarm, Sael's radiant Moonbeam, and two devastating Reckless flurries doing the bulk of it — after refusing an offer to hold off in exchange for the count's willing surrender (Kathrivex genuinely couldn't transfer it safely without Malphestor present). Darkhawk then held the ownerless anchor stable by hand for hours until Malphestor and Voreth arrived to complete the real transfer, closing the theft that began before he was even taken from Thundertree. Full-party milestone: Darkhawk, Mara, and Ysolde took Epic Boons (Combat Prowess x2, Recovery); Sael and Wren both hit character level 20/max.

A hidden vault beneath the anchor's dais gave up Ravask's real location (the Grey Vault, Stonewatch Hollow, western coast) and genuine doubt even Kathrivex held about Kellidra's loyalty. The Reckoning House is becoming Malphestor and Voreth's shared honest seat. The full council was briefed properly — real relief, tempered by Ysolde's raised (unproven) theory that the fist-and-chain sigil might mean Ravask or Kellidra held leverage *over* Kathrivex all along. A second Raven Queen audience resolved Voreth's standing finding (downgraded to historical fact, his role in ending the theft recognized as real). A new bond-feature, **The Bear's Mantle**, was discovered on the avatar armor.

The western scouting party reached the Grey Vault and, after careful reconnaissance (Scrying on a wild-shaped Sael revealed a rattled, genuinely conflicted Ravask being managed by an unknown authority above him), Rurik walked up to the front door and talked Ravask into real, lasting change — confessions, honest questions about mercy versus rage, and a direct challenge to reach out to Kellidra as an equal. **She accepted.** The honest-count structure is now four devils strong (Malphestor, Voreth, Ravask, Kellidra), pending a full meeting at the Reckoning House. A locked chest at the Vault revealed a genuinely new, larger mystery: an ancient charter naming **the First Auditor**, an authority claimed "above gods and devils both" and the true origin of the entire fist-and-chain hierarchy — real, per Ashworn, but of unknown identity or actual power. The party deliberately chose not to make contact yet, holding to the same patient-intelligence discipline that made the Reckoning House survivable.
