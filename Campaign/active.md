**Last session:** Session 44 — 2026-08-15 (Rest, a permanent gift, and the mountain keep breached. After Session 43's massive council, Rurik processed the weight of it honestly — the bear-spirit confirmed even it never knew the true scale of this fight; a dawn talk with Mara surfaced his deepest fear (letting everyone down) and her answer (showing up after is the only promise that matters). Real rest followed: Dain became a genuine, mutually-declared friend and agreed to brief Vosk's scouts; a sensing plan was agreed with Vosk; Sarna and Kell's lesson turned up old bones and a mystery goliath spear head; Alara's tonic and Joss's coastal trade both moved forward; Perrin and Greta secretly built Rurik a sky-iron shield, switching him to sword-and-shield ahead of the expedition. At the Standing Ring, Rurik asked Kethrun why the bear-spirit's protection never covered psychic damage — the answer led to **Kethrun's Ward**: naming a real, unspoken doubt (feeling unworthy to lead Urskelde) aloud in front of the whole party, and choosing to set it down, earning permanent psychic resistance. The expedition northeast proved the new **Travel/Camp Risk Tiers** tools: a boundary-marker construct ambush was caught first by the combined sensing plan and destroyed without real harm to the party. Reaching the keep, the party breached the main gate, found dozens of dormant constructs likely sharing one activation source, and discovered the keep's true nature climbing to a second level — an active **hub crystal** connecting a dozen-plus threads, some live and possibly human, two apparently running toward the other two priority sites. Rather than sever anything blind, Rurik chose to **occupy and hold the keep**. A floor search turned up the first real trace of who builds Kathrivex's infrastructure: a letter and personal effects naming an unidentified figure, **"R.,"** who treats even Kathrivex as a resource rather than a master. Pushing into the unexplored lower level, the party found and freed **Elenna**, held in magical stasis — wife of **Aldren Voss**, a trader drawn into the network by debt and stationed here before being moved north, alive, toward the burned court. Rurik told her everything; she confirmed real detail on R. and placed her hometown as a strong match for the alliance's coastal-town lead. Further exploration turned up a transfer ledger naming a brand new fourth site, **"the Reckoning House,"** an unidentified protective ring, and a second black scrying mirror — likely a direct line to R., untested. Pushing "deeper in," the party found the keep's actual construct-manufacturing workshop — a dozen-plus coerced human workers under two overseers. Rurik walked in alone and declared himself the new authority; the ensuing short fight killed one overseer and saw three more guards (**Corbett, Fenwick, Tamsin**) surrender. All three had their own accounts severed by Rurik at once — close enough to the hub this time that it reacted hard and visibly, the first time that's happened. The dozen-plus workers were freed but left unsevered out of caution. A final search turned up an unfinished, more advanced **Auditor-type construct prototype**, its notes signed off by a second unidentified figure, **"K.,"** distinct from R. — and confirmed **Keeper Ilsa**, absent three days and explaining the keep's thin security, left recently heading the same direction as Aldren Voss: toward the burned court. Halvard arrived, toured the whole keep, took full accounts from Elenna, Corbett, Fenwick, Tamsin, and all the freed workers, and named the shape of what this alliance now faces plainly: not just a god's network, but people — R. and K. at minimum — using it as a leveraged resource. Rather than return to brief the council in person, Rurik delegated the full report to Halvard and pushed the party straight toward the burned court, judging the lead too fresh to lose. **Corbett was placed in charge of the keep** under the same guarded-trust structure as Voss, Vosk's hunters patrolling independently. Pushing hard toward the burned court over the following days, the party found a trail of ritual "closing" burns (ledger pages destroyed to erase debts rather than free people) and closed in on a separate seven-person group heading the same direction. A tense standoff with their hooded, self-possessed leader — who works by choice, not debt, and pushed back hard on Rurik's judgment before Rurik met it with his own raw honesty — ended in a hard-fought, one-sided fight: Sael's Spike Growth and Wren's Chain Lightning devastated the escort (four dead), and **the leader was captured alive and unconscious**, per Rurik's explicit order to disable rather than kill. Three soldiers surrendered. Not one party member was seriously hurt. Interrogating the Leader afterward revealed the actual delivery point is a disguised waystation, not the burned court itself, that her employer pays the court **tribute** (two separate powers, not one hierarchy), and — from a token hidden on one of the dead — the first physical symbol tied to **"K.,"** distinct from R.'s mark. One soldier, offered a chance at revenge against his leader, refused it on principle and was freed outright; the rest travel to the keep restrained. Judging the intel too dangerous to push on blind (plus an unexplained ticking sound now coming from the Leader's locked cargo case), Rurik called off the direct assault and turned the column back to consolidate with Halvard first. Back at the keep, the case's failsafe ward was safely disarmed and opened with Halvard present — revealing a dozen crystallized fragments of actual severed human threads, tribute paid in literal pieces of people rather than coin. Rurik connected it to Malphestor and the Nine Hells: devils deal in souls, not debts, so "the count" was very likely built on genuine infernal soul-currency from the start — meaning no other power has ever opposed Kathrivex not out of ignorance, but because at least one (the Nine Hells) is being paid, in real souls, to look away. Halvard and Ysolde both confirmed the logic; it directly answers the "have other deities noticed" question left open since the council. **Malphestor is now the campaign's most urgent open thread** — possibly the only living witness to how deep that involvement runs.
