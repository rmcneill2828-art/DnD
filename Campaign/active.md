Rurik was carried home unconscious for five days, dreamed of Ashpit and a question from the Raven Queen and Thossia about what he actually wants for himself, and woke rested and whole to Mara and Ysolde bickering over his care. The full settlement council still only has the bare outline Mara gave at the gate — the real story is still owed.
