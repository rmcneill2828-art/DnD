Real rest followed: Dain became a genuine, mutually-declared friend and agreed to brief Vosk's scouts; a sensing plan was agreed with Vosk; Sarna and Kell's lesson turned up old bones and a mystery goliath spear head; Alara's tonic and Joss's coastal trade both moved forward; Perrin and Greta secretly built Rurik a sky-iron shield, switching him to sword-and-shield ahead of the expedition. At the Standing Ring, Rurik asked Kethrun why the bear-spirit's protection never covered psychic damage — the answer led to **Kethrun's Ward**: naming a real, unspoken doubt (feeling unworthy to lead Urskelde) aloud in front of the whole party, and choosing to set it down, earning permanent psychic resistance. The expedition northeast proved the new **Travel/Camp Risk Tiers** tools: a boundary-marker construct ambush was caught first by the combined sensing plan and destroyed without real harm to the party. Reaching the keep, the party breached the main gate, found dozens of dormant constructs likely sharing one activation source, and discovered the keep's true nature climbing to a second level — an active **hub crystal** connecting a dozen-plus threads, some live and possibly human, two apparently running toward the other two priority sites. Rather than sever anything blind, Rurik chose to **occupy and hold the keep**. A floor search turned up the first real trace of who builds Kathrivex's infrastructure: a letter and personal effects naming an unidentified figure, **"R.,"** who treats even Kathrivex as a resource rather than a master. Pushing into the unexplored lower level, the party found and freed **Elenna**, held in magical stasis — wife of **Aldren Voss**, a trader drawn into the network by debt and stationed here before being moved north, alive, toward the burned court. Rurik told her everything; she confirmed real detail on R. and placed her hometown as a strong match for the alliance's coastal-town lead. Further exploration turned up a transfer ledger naming a brand new fourth site, **"the Reckoning House,"** an unidentified protective ring, and a second black scrying mirror — likely a direct line to R., untested. Pushing "deeper in," the party found the keep's actual construct-manufacturing workshop — a dozen-plus coerced human workers under two overseers. Rurik walked in alone and declared himself the new authority; the ensuing short fight killed one overseer and saw three more guards (**Corbett, Fenwick, Tamsin**) surrender. All three had their own accounts severed by Rurik at once — close enough to the hub this time that it reacted hard and visibly, the first time that's happened. The dozen-plus workers were freed but left unsevered out of caution. A final search turned up an unfinished, more advanced **Auditor-type construct prototype**, its notes signed off by a second unidentified figure, **"K.,"** distinct from R. — and confirmed **Keeper Ilsa**, absent three days and explaining the keep's thin security, left recently heading the same direction as Aldren Voss: toward the burned court. Halvard arrived, toured the whole keep, took full accounts from Elenna, Corbett, Fenwick, Tamsin, and all the freed workers, and named the shape of what this alliance now faces plainly: not just a god's network, but people — R. and K. at minimum — using it as a leveraged resource. Rather than return to brief the council in person, Rurik delegated the full report to Halvard and pushed the party straight toward the burned court, judging the lead too fresh to lose. **Corbett was placed in charge of the keep** under the same guarded-trust structure as Voss, Vosk's hunters patrolling independently.
